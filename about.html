About Us

Our Purpose

To glorify God by making His Word and truth understandable, accessible, and practical for everyday life. We exist to help believers not only know what the Bible says, but understand its truth and faithfully live according to it, allowing God's Word to become the foundation for how they think, live, and walk with Christ.

Our Mission

Our Daily Bread exists to strengthen believers through the Word of God, helping them live in daily dependence on Him through Jesus Christ—the true Bread of Life. We equip Christians with Scripture so they are not spiritually starved, but built up in truth, grounded in faith, and prepared for every battle and every day of life.

Our Vision

To see Christians strengthened daily by God's Word, walking in steady faith, growing in spiritual maturity, and living in bold obedience. We desire to see believers sustained not by the temporary things of this world, but by Christ alone—knowing His Word, trusting His truth, and faithfully living it out in every area of life.
